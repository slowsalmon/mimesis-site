Who we are

Mimesis Studio is a painting app for iPad made by Slowsalmon. This policy covers the app and this website, mimesis.slowsalmon.com.

Your artworks and settings

Artworks, brushes you make, brush racks and app settings are saved only in the app’s storage on your iPad. They are processed entirely on the device. We have no copy of them and no way to see them.

You can see the app’s folder in the Files app. Deleting the app deletes this data from the iPad.

Photos

  • Choosing an image. When you start an artwork from an image, or add an underdrawing or a reference image, you pick it with the system photo picker. The app receives only the images you pick, and keeps them with that artwork on your iPad.
  • Saving to Photos. Saving a finished painting asks for permission to add photos only. With that permission the app can add images to your library but cannot read anything already in it.

You can change photo permissions at any time in the iPad’s Settings.

Files you export or share

An artwork leaves your iPad only when you export or share it yourself: image files, artwork files (.mime) and brush files (.mimbrush). They go wherever you send them through the share sheet or the Files app, and that service’s own privacy policy then applies.

Files you open in the app are read on the device and never sent anywhere.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPad to a computer, iPadOS may include the app’s data in that backup. Backups are made and kept by Apple or on your own computer under your settings. We have no access to them.

Purchase, App Store and Apple

Mimesis Studio is sold through the App Store as a single purchase. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Apple gives developers sales figures and, only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad’s settings, anonymous crash reports. These are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
